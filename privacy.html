Longxia 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-10-06

  1. Longxia 只供擁有者本人使用，只存取擁有者自己的 Google 帳號（Gmail、Google 日曆）。
  2. 讀取的郵件與行程只在擁有者自己的電腦上處理與保存，不會出售、出租或分享給任何第三方。
  3. 授權憑證加密存放在擁有者的電腦上；擁有者可隨時在 Google 帳戶的「第三方存取權」移除授權。
  4. Longxia 對 Google 使用者資料的使用遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策（含有限使用規定）。

Privacy Policy

  1. Longxia is used only by its owner and accesses only the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar).
  2. Email and calendar data are processed and stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  3. Authorization tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer; access can be revoked at any time in Google Account > Third-party access.
  4. Longxia's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
